Tips for Better Results

A few simple tips to get the best results from your images.

01

Resize with care

Use the dimensions you actually need. Keep the aspect ratio locked to prevent stretched or distorted images.

02

Balance size and quality

For photographs, start with around 80% quality when available. Compare the result and adjust until it looks good.

03

Choose the right format

JPEG works well for photographs, PNG is useful for transparency, and WebP is often a good choice for web images.

04

Crop before you resize

Remove unwanted areas first, then resize the remaining image to your preferred dimensions.

05

Compare before downloading

Check the final dimensions, file size, and visual quality before using your processed image.

06

Keep your original

Save a copy of your original image before editing, so you can start again without losing quality.
